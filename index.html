About Me

Henri is a 24-year old doctoral student of Geology at the University of Salzburg. He began his studies in October 2025 under the supervision of Jörg Robl in the Department of Geology. Originally from Britain, Henri has just completed a two-year Master's programme at the Vrije Universiteit in the Netherlands, where over the course of those two-years his interests led to a specialisation in tectonic geomorphology, particularly in New Zealand and Southern Calabria.
